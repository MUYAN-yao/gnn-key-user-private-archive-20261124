## Admitted Work

Yang, Zhu, Zhang, Diao, and Bai, "Influence Maximization in Temporal Social
Networks with the Mixed K-Shell Method," *Electronics* 13(13), 2533 (2024),
DOI `10.3390/electronics13132533`.

- Crossref and OpenAlex agree on title, DOI, authors, venue, and year.
- DOI content negotiation supplied the BibTeX entry retained in
  `manuscript/references.bib`.
- The publisher's 16-page CC BY PDF was retrieved and inspected. It uses a
  degree/k-shell/temporal-k-shell MKS heuristic and evaluates seed sets under
  ICT diffusion with 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations on four temporal social
  interaction datasets.
- The text and matrix distinguish its whole-graph structural ranking and ICT
  evaluation from this study's historical/future isolation, GATv2 candidate
  screening, common-random-number evaluator, and risk-coverage objective.
