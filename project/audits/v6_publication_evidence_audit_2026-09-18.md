# v6 publication-evidence audit

## Admission decision

The v6 Digg k=5 formal batch is admitted for the narrow claims below. Its ten
formal artifacts passed read-only validation of input hashes, source snapshot,
protocol copy, run hashes, configuration, deterministic disjoint worlds,
metric rows and formal eligibility. The preflight is excluded from outcome
analysis.

## Supported claims

1. Under the stated retrospective Digg simulation protocol, GNN-ranked pools
   of 60 and 120 candidates reproduced full-candidate robust greedy search in
   all ten stored paired blocks.
2. The tested 30-candidate GNN pool lost primary robust objective in all ten
   blocks, with a negative paired interval and Holm-adjusted secondary p value
   of 0.00586.
3. The recorded arm-specific search-stage time was much lower for the
   preserved 60/120 pools than for full search, subject to the stated shared
   preprocessing exclusion.
4. A 120-node weighted-out-degree pool also reproduced full search, so the
   data do not support an independent GNN-over-degree screening claim.

## Claims that remain blocked

- Statistical equivalence from exact ties.
- Learned-prior superiority over weighted degree.
- Causal diffusion or intervention benefit.
- Strictly online prediction or deployment validity.
- End-to-end acceleration, because common preprocessing is excluded.
- Generality beyond the two conditional network protocols and their fixed
  budgets, simulators and data treatment.

## Submission consequence

The new result closes the planned independent-network and larger-budget check
for the candidate-reduction main line. It does not close manuscript readiness:
the full English paper, figures and captions, final claim ledger,
author-provided declarations and public reproducibility snapshot remain
required. No further training is required for the narrower candidate-reduction
manuscript. If the paper instead retains a learned-prior efficacy headline,
add a separately declared same-budget GNN-versus-degree attribution study
before submission.

## Decision

Proceed by integrating the admitted v6 evidence and revising the title,
abstract and discussion around candidate reduction and its failure boundary.
Do not run an adaptive experiment merely to seek a positive learned-prior
comparison.
