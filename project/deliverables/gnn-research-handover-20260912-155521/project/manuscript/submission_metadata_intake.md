## Completion Rule

Before official-template conversion, every field must have a verified value or
an explicit, author-confirmed "not applicable" decision. Set the Status column
to exactly `confirmed` or `not applicable`, and retain a nonempty confirmation
source for every row; the final audit rejects blank values and informal status
labels. The actual current template must then be re-downloaded and checked,
because field labels and ordering may differ from the 2025 sample identified in
`manuscript/venue_selection.md`.
