- [ ] Format the 34-entry bibliography as consecutive numeric references and

- [ ] Produce editable LaTeX source using the requested Springer formatting

## Author-controlled material

- [ ] Supply author names, affiliations, corresponding-author e-mail and any
  available ORCIDs on the title page.
- [ ] Confirm all authors approve the work and that it is not submitted or
  under consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Supply funding, acknowledgements, author-contribution and competing-
  interest statements under **Statements and Declarations**.
- [ ] Confirm ethics/consent applicability.
- [ ] Provide a data-availability statement for this original research article.
  It must explain access to the provider-hosted raw data and the approved
  archive for the reproducibility materials.
- [ ] Confirm the wording used to document any generative-AI assistance. The
  journal distinguishes copy editing from generative content creation and
  requires appropriate documentation of the latter in Methods or an equivalent
  manuscript section, with human author accountability for the final text.

## Route and eligibility

- [ ] Submit as a regular Original Paper, outside special issues, collections,
  supplements and conference tracks.
- [ ] Select **subscription publishing** after acceptance. The current official
  page states that this route has no APC; OA is optional and currently carries
  an APC.
- [ ] Recheck current journal instructions, indexing and the university's
  recognition policy immediately before the authors submit.
