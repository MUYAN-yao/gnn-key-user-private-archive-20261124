## Why this journal

*Applied Intelligence* is a hybrid journal whose regular subscription route
has no article processing charge. Its official journal page lists both EI
Compendex and Science Citation Index Expanded (SCIE). Under the school's
current policy, a regular published Article therefore meets the SCI path for
C-level recognition at minimum; a higher JCR classification, if applicable in
the publication year, would take precedence. The paper's use of GATv2,
temporal-network learning and influence-maximization decision search directly
matches the journal's stated focus on AI, neural networks and real complex
problems.

Submit only as a regular **Original Paper** through the subscription route.
Do not choose Open Access, special issue, collection, supplement or conference
track. The latter formats can alter the school-recognition outcome even when
the journal itself is indexed.

## Proposed positioning

**Working title:** *Candidate Reduction in Temporal Key-User Identification:
A Protocol-Conditional Audit of Preservation, Cost, and Failure Boundaries*

The paper should lead with a practical AI decision question: whether a learned
temporal diffusion prior can constrain a fixed robust-greedy search without
changing the selected seed set under the stated protocol. It should then report
the observed conditional preservation, search-stage cost record and failure
limits. It must not claim end-to-end acceleration, causal diffusion validation,
learned-prior superiority over weighted degree, universal dominance, or an
online deployment guarantee.

The v5 result is a resource-feasibility limit, not an additional comparative
result. The formal k=5 full-candidate experiment was not admitted because the
validated Higgs preflight exceeded its preregistered runtime gate. Retain this
statement in limitations or reproducibility material, rather than turning it
into an efficacy result.
