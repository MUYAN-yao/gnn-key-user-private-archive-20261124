retaining domestic and international work collected during the earlier
Software Journal preparation. It is not a
benchmark table and makes no cross-paper performance claim. “Not reported”

`citation_audit.md`; it does **not** prove that a full paper lacks it.

## Allowed manuscript differentiation

- The paper combines a time-windowed candidate prior with a robust,
  cross-community greedy objective and separates influence optimization from
  future-edge validation.
- The related works above motivate a comparison of method scopes; they do not
  establish superiority of the present method outside its own protocols.
