## Recommended submission framing

Submit as a **computational-methods / reproducibility study in social-network
optimization**, with the stated title and bounded contribution. Do not frame it
as a new GNN method, a candidate-pruning algorithm, an information-diffusion
study, or a claim that degree screening improves influence. Those versions are
not supported by the evidence.

## Cost and indexing screen, checked 2026-09-20

Social Network Analysis and Mining is a topical fit and lists EI Compendex, but
it is now fully open access. Its publisher lists a current APC of £2,290. That
exceeds the stated ¥10,000 ceiling by a wide margin, so it is **not** a
submission target under the user's cost constraint.

The revised first target is *Computing*: its official scope expressly includes
network science, social networks and collective intelligence; it is hybrid,
lists EI Compendex and SCIE, has a six-day median first-decision metric, and
offers a no-APC subscription route. Frame the paper as a rigorous,
reproducible evaluation of candidate-truncated simulation-based network
optimization. *Applied Intelligence* remains the second target, but its focus
on original intelligent-systems research creates more desk-rejection risk for a
methods-and-reproducibility paper. The full, current screen is in
`venue_shortlist_2026-09-20.md`.

No journal-status statement substitutes for the university's publication-year
recognition rule. The corresponding author should confirm that a regular
subscription Original Article in the selected venue satisfies that policy
immediately before submitting.

## Author actions required before submission

1. Insert author names, affiliations, corresponding-author e-mail and ORCIDs.
2. Confirm author order, authorship approval, funding, conflicts, contribution
   statement, ethics/consent applicability and a truthful AI-use statement.
3. Create and inspect a public archival repository release, then insert its
   DOI/URL in the data/code statement. Do not include the raw provider archives
   until the authors deliberately confirm the redistribution plan.
4. Recheck the target journal's current author instructions, article type,
   indexing and publishing route on the day of submission.
5. Format the manuscript in Computing's required one-column LaTeX template
   (smallextended, at most 25 pages); Word is accepted only by exception,
   preserving the final figure as vector PDF/300-dpi PNG and retaining the
   condition-level supplementary CSV.
