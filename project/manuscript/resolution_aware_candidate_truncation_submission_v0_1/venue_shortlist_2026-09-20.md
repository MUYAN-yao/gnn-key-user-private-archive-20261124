# Cost-constrained venue shortlist for the resolution-aware candidate-truncation paper

**Checked:** 2026-09-20. This screen applies the supplied Lanzhou University
Artificial Intelligence School policy: an SCIE journal qualifies as C class,
which is above the requested D-class floor; EI/ESCI journal articles qualify as
D class. It applies only to an original research article with Lanzhou University
as the first affiliation and does not replace the school's publication-year
determination.

## Interpretation of the time requirement

No reputable journal can promise acceptance, peer-review completion, or online
publication in one to three months. The publisher metrics below are **median
submission-to-first-decision** figures. A rapid first decision may be an
editorial rejection; a revise-and-resubmit or acceptance normally takes longer.

## Not recommended for the current manuscript

- *Social Network Analysis and Mining* is the best topical match but is fully
  open access, with a stated APC of £2,290. It does not meet the non-OA/
  ¥10,000 constraint.
- *International Journal of Machine Learning and Cybernetics* and *Neural
  Computing and Applications* meet the indexing, hybrid and speed filters, but
  the current manuscript does not make a machine-learning or neural-computing
  contribution. Submitting there would require changing the evidence-faithful
  research question, so they are excluded rather than used merely for speed.
- *The Journal of Supercomputing* meets the indexing, subscription and 38-day
  first-decision filters, but its core scope is high-performance computing.
  The present experiment records decision cost but does not offer a
  supercomputing, parallelization or systems contribution.
- *Journal of Computer Science and Technology* meets the school threshold but
  its publicly reported review duration is about six months, outside the stated
  timing goal.

## Recommended submission order

1. **Computing**, regular article, subscription route. Revise the title and
   cover letter to lead with reproducible evaluation of candidate-truncated
   simulation-based network optimization.
2. If declined without a revision opportunity, use the same evidence-faithful
   manuscript for **Applied Intelligence**, with the intelligent decision-system
   reliability framing.
3. Use **Knowledge and Information Systems** only after narrowing the framing
   to an information-system evaluation protocol.

Never submit to more than one journal at once. Recheck indexing, price and the
subscription selection at acceptance, because publisher policy and school
classification can change.
