# Claim ledger: simulation-resolution-aware candidate truncation

## Claims permitted after a complete, validated confirmation matrix

1. In the stated IC simulator, a candidate pool can reproduce the **same-W
   finite-world full-greedy reference** on a named network instance.
   Evidence: complete matrix condition file, ordered seed trace, nested-world
   trajectory, and validator-passed source/input provenance.
2. Increasing the selection-world budget can change full-greedy seed sequences,
   tie counts, margins, and pool agreement on specified instances.
   Evidence: all three nested W conditions for each path, with failures shown.
3. Candidate-pool quality--cost comparisons should report selection resolution,
   independent evaluation and decision cost jointly.
   Evidence: pre-registered matrix plus the complete descriptive profile.

## Claims permanently prohibited

- “GNN is better than degree screening.”
- “A 4k pool is universally equivalent to full greedy.”
- “The contact data observe information cascades or causal social influence.”
- Population p-values or general causal claims from the four contact instances.
- A new approximation algorithm, a new safe-pruning theorem, or an exact
  full-search contribution.

## Required manuscript labels

- The primary task is **simulated IC on contact-derived bidirectional
  topologies**.
- Every pool-agreement result names W and the world path.
- Development Digg/Higgs results remain separate from the confirmation matrix.
- Failed, stopped and non-admitted runs remain outside all tables and figures.
