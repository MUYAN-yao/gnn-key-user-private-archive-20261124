2.2 Finite-world coupling counterexample
Consider the chains→u→vwith horizon two. In one step-indexed world,s→uis
open at step zero, whileu→vis closed at step zero and open at step one. Starting
with{s}reaches all three vertices. Starting with{s,u}activatesuearlier and reaches
only two. Thus the finite-world objective is not necessarily monotone, even though
the marginal law for each fixed seed set remains the intended independent-cascade
law. This example alone does not establish a violation of submodularity. The fixed-
edge control instead uses horizon-limited reachability in each sampled graph, giving
a monotone, submodular coverage objective. Both implementations enumerate all
eligible vertices at every round. The external world-ID audit checks 169,200 distinct
identifiers across graph, path and selection/evaluation families for collisions.
3 Selection and evaluation budgets
In the external budget sweeps and the 520-condition historical extension, every arm
selects its seeds using the sameWworlds within a condition. Full and restricted selec-
tions are recomputed at eachW; candidate pools are fixed across budgets for a given
ranking setting. Each graph/path/coupling uses a separateM=10,000-world eval-
uation family, fixed across arms and budgets. The initial 42-condition study instead
usedM=2000, as recorded above.
Ranking tolerance and pool cutoff govern candidate construction, separately from
WandM. At a fixedW, the post-hoc precision control recomputes only restricted
arms and reuses the verified full-reference result at that budget. AcrossW, the com-
parator is the corresponding budget-specific full reference. Restricted-minus-full dif-
ferences and higher-minus-lower-budget full-reference transitions are distinct com-
parisons.

4 Statistical interpretation
For a fixed selected pair, conditional Monte Carlo standard error is the sample stan-
dard deviation of 10,000 paired per-world differences divided by 100. Normal in-
tervals are pointwise and conditional. Across-path SD describes five computational
paths, or three control paths, and includes finite evaluation noise. The optionaltinter-
vals in CSVs are post-hoc descriptive intervals, not population-of-network inference.
Random pools are averaged within each path before cross-path analysis; their shared-
world covariance is retained.
For a seed set of sizek, incremental spread is total spread minusk. The exter-
nal experiments usek=5; the historical extension also includesk=20. The paired
difference is restricted minus same-budget full-reference spread. Percentage loss is
minus the paired mean difference divided by full incremental spread, times 100. The
plug-in percentage does not have a guaranteed loss-bound interpretation. The pri-
mary sign-test family is three methods within each graph atW=1280. All nonzero
PageRank and random-mean differences are negative, but five paths limit the min-
imum two-sided exact sign-test value to .0625. Nonsignificance is not equivalence.
Table S2 reports all nine tests without exclusions.
5 Cost and repeated measurements
The archived preparation timer contains parsing, all rankings, hashes and graph di-
agnostics. Its ranking subtimers are not added to it again. A separate post-hoc setup
study measures five repetitions of raw parsing/CSR and one method’s ranking plus
pool generation. The frozen selection timer already includes lexical candidate sort-
ing; it is not charged again. The assembled selection-use cost adds that median setup
to frozen selection-world preparation and selection. The assembled cost including
independent evaluation adds independent-world preparation and independent evalua-
tion. These are assembled warm-process, cached-file estimates, not direct end-to-end
trials. Imports, JIT compilation, downloads and output writing are excluded.
Frozen repeated timing uses path 1101 andW=320, five randomized repetitions
per arm, yielding 130 observations. Selected sequences and per-world evaluations
were required to remain identical. The CSVs report every repetition and its selec-
tion/evaluation time, plus medians and ranges. The maximum positive psutil process-
lifetime peak is 3.448822 GiB. Preflight used one tenth of the evaluation-world count
and therefore has a different resource scope; the recorded peak is not a condition-
specific allocation maximum. Timings are machine- and implementation-specific.

8 Post-hoc precision–budget control
The initial precision control left a material question open because it covered onlyW=
1280. A new protocol was frozen before new utility outputs were inspected and added
PageRank-4k and PageRank-5% arms at the tighter tolerance atW=80,320 for all
three graphs and five original paths. It contributes 30 conditions and 60 restricted
arms, reusing the frozen full reference for each corresponding budget and disjoint
evaluation-world IDs. Together with the prior tighter-toleranceW=1280 layer, this
is a 45-condition, 90-arm post-hoc sensitivity study; it adds no independent paths or
primary tests.
Candidate membership agrees between 10 −12 and 10 −14 for every graph/pool
size. This checks membership stability at the tested cutoffs, not exact PageRank-
vector convergence. CollegeMsg’s tighter- and default-tolerance pools are identi-
cal. ca-GrQc changes two of 20 4k candidates (pool Jaccard .818), while wiki-V ote
changes one (Jaccard .905). The lower-budget results show that ca-GrQc’s default
PageRank-4k direction reversal disappears with the tighter-tolerance pool: incremen-
tal losses are -11.25%, -2.96% and -0.54% atW=80,320,1280. Its selected sets and
paired utility magnitude still change with budget. CollegeMsg retains 9.33%, 8.52%
and 9.01% 4k losses at the tighter tolerance; wiki-V ote retains 74.05%, 74.05% and
73.96%. These graph-specific, post-hoc sensitivity findings use paired effect sizes
and path variation as described in the statistical interpretation.

12.1 Inputs, identifiers and numerical environment
The external package root containsconfig.json,protocol.md,snapshot/,
data/source_manifest.jsonandresults/main/. The default study has 54
14 Jiayue Sun et al.
JSON/NPZ pairs and 468 arms. The two post-hoc controls remain underreview-v2/
andreview-v3-budget-precision/; they contain 15 and 30 conditions, respec-
tively, with two restricted arms per condition. These outputs must not be merged
with the main-study count.
The namespace iscomputing-external-validation-20261015-v1. For
datasetd, pathr, familyfand world indexi, the exact UTF-8 key is
namespace|dataset|path|family|i
using decimal integers without padding. The world ID is the first eight SHA-256
bytes, interpreted as an unsigned big-endian integer and masked to 63 bits. Families
areselectionandevaluation; indices start at zero. World-to-edge draws use the
frozen unsigned-64-bit mixing function inlegacy_engine.py, with a 53-bit uni-
form output. In the step coupling the step index enters this function; the fixed-edge
coupling uses step zero throughout. Replacing this mapping with a different pseudo-
random generator would not reproduce the archived paths.
Random-pool keys arenamespace|pool|dataset|path|repeat, with repeti-
tion indices 0, 1 and 2. Their first eight SHA-256 bytes, without the 63-bit mask, seed
NumPy’sdefault_rng; for the externalk=5 study, the first 20 entries of a node
permutation form the pool. Greedy evaluation sorts these candidate indices before
scanning them. Lexicographic node indexing makes the first integer-score maximizer
the specified tie winner. PageRank uses the graph insertion order generated by the
frozen reader, so reproductions retain that order as well as its tolerance.
Core dependencies are pinned inrequirements.txt; the checked environment
is Python 3.13.0 on Windows. Pillow 12.3.0 is additionally used for decoded-pixel
comparison. TeX Live 2026 and Poppler support manuscript compilation and render-
ing, but are unnecessary for the propagation calculation. The reproduction wrapper
sets OpenMP, OpenBLAS, MKL and Numba thread limits to one before numerical
imports. Its environment record reports the actual installed versions for each run.
12.2 Commands and acceptance criteria
The retained external reproduction guide,EXTERNAL_REPRODUCE_INHERITED.md,
gives PowerShell commands for an isolated environment, input acquisition and
the two replay modes. The currentREPRODUCE.mdseparately describes regen-
eration of historical summaries without simulation. For external replay, run
src/reproduce.pyfrom the revised package with explicit–baseand a fresh
–output. The base is the external package root; the output must not exist. The script
checks original source, raw-file, processed-graph and main-result hashes before
computation. It preserves the source tree and builds a working copy for legacy
analysis scripts whose output paths are fixed relative to their own locations.
Therepresentativemode reruns path 1101 for all three graphs atW=80 with
step coupling andW=320 with fixed-edge coupling. It also reruns PageRank with
the tighter tolerance atW=80,1280 on that path, covering both precision protocols.
Theallmode reruns all 54 main conditions and all 45 post-hoc precision conditions.
Thenonemode checks saved results and regenerates analysis without rerunning seed

selection or propagation; it still reconstructs graphs and candidate pools and checks
world identifiers. Raw inputs are required in all modes because graph hashes and
direction diagnostics are reconstructed.
A replay passes only if selected IDs, every integer candidate score and every one
of the 10,000 evaluation counts match their archived counterparts exactly. The anal-
ysis stage independently recomputes paired differences and Monte Carlo standard
errors from saved arrays, applies the frozen aggregation and test rules, and regener-
ates the default and precision plots. CSV values are compared at absolute and relative
tolerance 10−12; decoded PNG pixels must be identical. The machine-readable report

data and SVG metadata are not equality targets. Cross-platform font rendering may
fail the pixel check even when numerical tables agree; such a difference requires
inspection rather than silent acceptance.

13.5 Traceability and scope
The historical tables and figures are generated from saved results and metadata by
src/integrate_historical.py. All-method CSVs retain arm metrics, within-
path random means, group summaries, candidate coverage and reference transitions;
figure-source CSVs contain every displayed value.REPRODUCE.mdrecords the ag-
gregation commands and checks.
Historical controls describe sensitivity on the tested contact networks; parameter
coverage on the external networks remains narrow. Their descriptive summaries nei-
ther add independent external paths nor change the post-hoc identity of the PageRank
control. The cost comparisons retain the exhaustive full-candidate reference used in
the experiments.
14 File map and availability
The full reproducibility materials comprise frozen sources and protocols, manifests,
external and historical results, per-path analyses and manuscript assets. Historical
aggregation scripts, source CSVs and verification records accompany the manuscript;
frozen experiment packages remain separate. Raw third-party inputs are obtained
through the guarded download instructions and are not redistributed. No public
archive URL is asserted.
