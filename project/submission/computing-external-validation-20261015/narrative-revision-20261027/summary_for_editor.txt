Title: Reference Budgets and Ranking Precision in Candidate-Restricted Influence Maximization

Contribution and scope
This evaluation study examines two numerical choices that enter candidate-restricted greedy influence maximization at different stages: the sampled-world budget defining the full-candidate reference and the precision of the ranking defining seed eligibility. Its empirical contribution is to identify a precision dependence in an apparent budget-induced quality reversal, relate reference-set changes to independently evaluated utility, and delimit the transfer of candidate-restriction results across observed topologies.

Findings
On ca-GrQc, default PageRank-4k changes from a mean incremental-spread gain of 5.61% at W=80 to a loss of 5.07% at W=1280. A paired post-hoc tolerance control removes this reversal while leaving budget-dependent seed changes. CollegeMsg and wiki-Vote retain losses under stricter ranking. Between W=320 and W=1280, all five ca-GrQc full-reference sets change, with a mean independent spread gain of 0.12720 nodes. At W=1280, 20-node degree-based pools reproduce all 15 external full-reference trajectories; measured selection savings are 98.40–99.67% relative to exhaustive full-candidate selection in the same implementation.

Evaluation
The frozen external matrix comprises three observed topologies, five computational paths, selection budgets 80/320/1280, shared selection-world prefixes and 10,000 disjoint evaluation worlds. The setting fixes k=5, p=.01 and horizon 3. Fixed-edge controls, candidate coverage and explicit timing scopes qualify the main comparisons. The 45 post-hoc precision conditions reuse the original paths and do not enlarge the independent sample. Historical contact-graph findings remain a separate evidence layer.

Interpretation and reproducibility
The study contributes evidence about how to interpret restriction quality, with separate reporting of trajectory agreement, set agreement, conditional Monte Carlo uncertainty, path variation and computational cost. The direction diagnostic is observational; reference agreement does not establish expected-optimality or equivalence. Frozen sources, input hashes, per-world outputs and analysis scripts support inspection. The 26 October reproduction check regenerated the external analyses and replayed 12 representative conditions (64 arms); this narrative revision preserves that record without claiming a full rerun or cross-platform replication. Raw third-party datasets are obtained from their cited providers and are not redistributed.
