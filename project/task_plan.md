# Publication Evidence Strengthening Plan

## Latest decision: 2026-09-20 simulation-resolution pivot

The admissible publication direction is now a computational-methods study on
**simulation-resolution-aware candidate truncation in influence maximization**.
It studies when a candidate-pool comparison is interpretable at all, rather
than proposing GNN pruning or claiming that any ranking heuristic is better.

The frozen local v9 development diagnostic completed all 16 conditions. Across
all eight matched `(dataset, seed, k)` blocks, re-optimizing full greedy from
the nested W=20 worlds to W=80 changed the ordered seed sequence. Full-search
zero-margin rounds fell from 45/100 at W=20 to 19/100 at W=80, but did not
disappear. Within the W=80 reference, degree-4k and degree-8k reproduced the
finite-world full sequence in 8/8 local blocks; degree-2k did so in 5/8.
These are development findings only, not population claims and not evidence of
GNN superiority.

The next publication gate is a pre-registered confirmation layer with legally
admitted external data plus a fixed W={20,80,320} resolution path. Until that
is complete, no manuscript may call the W=80 pool threshold universal or
describe this project as submission-ready. The detailed evidence reading is in
`analysis/candidate_truncation_resolution_development_v0_1_readout.md`; the
research card is
`protocols/simulation_resolution_aware_candidate_truncation_research_card_v0_1.md`.
