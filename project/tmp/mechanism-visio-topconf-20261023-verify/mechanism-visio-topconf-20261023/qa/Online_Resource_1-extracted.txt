2 Audit and reproducibility
There are 54 configuration-defined external conditions and 468 executed arms. The
archive retains one JSON and NPZ pair per condition, source and data manifests,
world-ID hashes, candidate IDs, selected sequences, all round/candidate integer
totals and 10,000 evaluation values per arm. Reproduction checks the raw gzip
hashes before using the frozen runner. The independent audit parses topology,
checks all recorded hashes and world IDs, reconstructs all argmax/tie decisions,
replays every full-candidate score matrix and all evaluation arrays, and checks a
scalar Python oracle on the first 16 worlds for each distinct ordered selection within
a graph/path/coupling. Complete replay and the independent sampled oracle are
different validation layers.
Frozen source files are unchanged. The original runner’s entry checks did not
enforce actual raw hashes against the data manifest; the retrospective comparison
and new guarded wrapper address that enforcement gap without rewriting history. ca-
GrQc removes loops before collecting endpoints: raw vertex 12295 appears only in a
loop, explaining 5,241 rather than 5,242 vertices. The original prose was ambiguous
about endpoint collection order, while the processed size/hash were already frozen
before outcomes. Results refer to that processed graph.
Direction is part of the simulation definition. CollegeMsg goes from sender to re-
ceiver; wiki-Vote from voter to votee; ca-GrQc uses reciprocal arcs. The first two are

3 Statistical interpretation
For a fixed selected pair, conditional Monte Carlo standard error is the sample stan-
dard deviation of 10,000 paired per-world differences divided by 100. Normal in-
tervals are pointwise and conditional. Across-path SD describes five computational
paths, or three control paths, and includes finite evaluation noise. The optional t inter-
vals in CSVs are post-hoc descriptive intervals, not population-of-network inference.
Random pools are averaged within each path before cross-path analysis; their shared-
world covariance is retained.
Incremental spread subtracts five seeds from total spread. Percentage loss is mi-
nus the paired mean difference divided by full incremental spread, times 100. The
plug-in percentage does not have a guaranteed loss-bound interpretation. The pri-
mary sign-test family is three methods within each graph at W = 1280. All nonzero
PageRank and random-mean differences are negative, but five paths limit the min-
imum two-sided exact sign-test value to .0625. Nonsignificance is not equivalence.
Table 2 reports all nine tests without exclusions.
4 Cost and repeated measurements
The archived preparation timer contains parsing, all rankings, hashes and graph di-
agnostics. Its ranking subtimers are not added to it again. A separate post-hoc setup
study measures five repetitions of raw parsing/CSR and one method’s ranking plus
pool generation. The frozen selection timer already includes lexical candidate sort-
ing; it is not charged again. Hybrid deployment cost adds that median setup to frozen
selection-world preparation and selection. Hybrid verification cost adds independent-
world preparation and independent evaluation. These are assembled warm-process,
cached-file estimates, not direct end-to-end trials. Imports, JIT compilation, down-
loads and output writing are excluded.
Frozen repeated timing uses path 1101 and W = 320, five randomized repetitions
per arm, yielding 130 observations. Selected sequences and per-world evaluations
were required to remain identical. The CSVs report every repetition and its selec-
tion/evaluation time, plus medians and ranges. The maximum positive psutil process-
lifetime peak is 3.448822 GiB; it must not be confused with an unverified PowerShell
display or with preflight measurements using one tenth the evaluation world count.
Timings are machine- and implementation-specific.

6 Post-hoc PageRank precision control
The frozen default tolerance is 10−6, and NetworkX stops on a successive vector L1
change below n times that value. A candidate-only diagnostic at 10−12 changed some
pool members on ca-GrQc and wiki-Vote; pools agreed between 10−12 and 10−14.
Before inspecting new utility outputs, a separate protocol froze two PageRank arms
on every graph and all five original W = 1280 paths. Its 15 conditions and 30 arms
are a numerical sensitivity control, not independent confirmation and not additional
paths in the primary tests.
Table 6 shows that tightening tolerance changes ca-GrQc’s 4k mean difference
from −0.25082 to +0.02608, replacing all five selected sets. Its original small loss
is therefore not precision-robust. CollegeMsg outcomes are unchanged. wiki-Vote’s
4k loss remains large (−41.21970, or 73.96% of incremental spread), and its 5% loss
is 24.47%. This diagnostic qualifies the ranking interpretation without replacing any
original observation or retroactively redefining the frozen sign-test family. Tightened-
solver timing is not substituted into the default-solver cost curves.

7 Post-hoc precision–budget control
The initial precision control left a material question open because it covered only
W = 1280. A new protocol was frozen before new utility outputs were inspected and
added strict PageRank-4k and PageRank-5% arms at W = 80,320 for all three graphs
and five original paths. It contributes 30 conditions and 60 restricted arms, reusing the
frozen full references and disjoint evaluation-world IDs. Together with the prior strict
W = 1280 layer, this is a 45-condition, 90-arm post-hoc sensitivity study; it adds no
independent paths or primary tests.
Strict 10−12 pools agree with 10−14 pools for every graph/pool size. CollegeMsg’s
strict and default pools are identical. ca-GrQc changes two of 20 4k candidates (pool
Jaccard .818), while wiki-Vote changes one (Jaccard .905). The lower-budget results
show that ca-GrQc’s default PageRank-4k direction reversal does not survive the sta-
ble strict pool: strict losses are -11.25%, -2.96% and -0.54% at W = 80,320,1280.
Its selected sets and paired utility magnitude still change with budget. CollegeMsg
retains 9.33%, 8.52% and 9.01% strict 4k losses; wiki-Vote retains 74.05%, 74.05%
and 73.96%. These are graph-specific, post-hoc sensitivity findings, not an equiva-
lence, superiority or significance claim.
