## Computing submission checks

Official guide: https://link.springer.com/journal/607/submission-guidelines
Scope: https://link.springer.com/journal/607/aims-and-scope

Use one-column smallextended LaTeX, at most 25 pages without compression;
150–250 abstract words; 4–6 keywords. Supply novelty/evaluation summary,
fit-focused cover letter, editable sources, institutional contact, applicable
declarations and interface contribution/conflict entries. Source files should not
require subfolders. Author approval and public archive details remain author-controlled.
AI-assisted work beyond language editing needs an accurate disclosure.

The study fits the named network-science/social-network subject area. These formal
checks do not establish sufficient novelty or imply editorial acceptance.
